Build an AI: Idle Game — Privacy Policy

App: Build an AI: Idle Game (com.droid.pocketmine) · Last updated: October 25, 2026

Build an AI: Idle Game is an offline Android game with optional on-device AI chat. This policy explains what data the app handles.

Data stored on your device

The app stores your game progress, AI name, settings, and, if you enable chat, your recent conversation in private app storage. The bundled AI model runs locally on your device. Chat text and game progress are not sent to us or to an AI server. Turning chat off clears its conversation; you can also use New chat to clear it. Uninstalling the app or clearing its Android app data removes locally stored data.

Optional AI reply reports

If you tap Report last AI reply and confirm Send report, the selected AI reply, any note you write, and the app version are sent over HTTPS to FormSubmit, a third-party form delivery service, which forwards the report to our support email. We use reports to review and improve AI safety. Other chat messages, your game progress, and your AI name are not included. FormSubmit may process network information such as your IP address to deliver the report. See FormSubmit’s privacy policy. Do not include personal information in your report.

Request data deletion

To request deletion of an AI reply report, email easyweathertask@gmail.com with the subject “Build an AI report deletion.” Include the approximate date and time you sent it and a short excerpt so we can locate the report. We will delete the matching report from our support mailbox. We do not have access to other chat messages, your AI name, or game progress, so those cannot be deleted through an email request. To delete that data stored on your device, use Android’s Clear storage for this app or uninstall it. FormSubmit may retain processing records under its own policy; contact them about those records.

Other data collection and sharing

Except for reports you choose to send, we do not collect or receive your personal information. We do not sell report data. The app does not require an account, request location or contacts, display ads, use analytics, or make in-app purchases. Its fictional “Internet Access” upgrade does not connect to the internet. The app requests Android's Internet permission only to send a report when you choose to submit one. The game and AI chat themselves work offline.

Children

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app's chat is generated on-device and is not monitored by us.

Changes

We may update this policy if the app changes. The date above shows the latest update.
