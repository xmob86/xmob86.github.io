Build an AI: Idle Game — Privacy Policy

App: Build an AI: Idle Game (com.droid.pocketmine) · Last updated: October 25, 2026

Build an AI: Idle Game is an offline Android game with optional on-device AI chat. This policy explains what data the app handles.

Data stored on your device

The app stores your game progress, AI name, settings, and, if you enable chat, your recent conversation in private app storage. The bundled AI model runs locally on your device. Chat text and game progress are not sent to us or to an AI server. Turning chat off clears its conversation; you can also use New chat to clear it. Uninstalling the app or clearing its Android app data removes locally stored data.

Data collection and sharing

We do not collect, receive, sell, or share your personal information. The app does not require an account, request location or contacts, display ads, use analytics, or make in-app purchases. Its fictional “Internet Access” upgrade does not connect to the internet. The app does not request Android's Internet permission.

Children

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app's chat is generated on-device and is not monitored by us.

Changes

We may update this policy if the app changes. The date above shows the latest update.
